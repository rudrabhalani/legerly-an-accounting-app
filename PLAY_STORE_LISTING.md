## 2. Google Play Store Full Description (4000 characters max)

> **Ledgerly: Simple Accounting for Everyone.**  
> Record your shop’s daily income, expenses, party khata, invoices, and inventory in under 10 seconds — with zero tutorial needed!

Are you a shopkeeper, retail trader, distributor, or service provider looking for a clean, light, and beautiful alternative to complicated accounting software?

Meet **Ledgerly**, the modern bookkeeping app built specifically for Indian businesses. Built on pure double-entry accounting principles behind the scenes, Ledgerly replaces confusing debit/credit jargon with crystal-clear **"Money In"** and **"Money Out"**.

### 🌟 Why 1,00,000+ Indian Shopkeepers Love Ledgerly:

#### ⚡ Record Transactions in Under 10 Seconds
- **+ Money In (Emerald Green):** Record customer receipts via Cash, UPI, NEFT, Cheque, or direct bank deposit instantly.
- **− Money Out (Coral Red):** Track vendor payments, shop rent, electricity, transport, and staff wages.
- **Smart Safeguards:** Alerts you before cash goes below zero.

#### 👥 Smart Party Khata & Customer Ledgers
- Always know **"You'll Get ₹"** from customers and **"You'll Give ₹"** to suppliers at a single glance.
- View detailed passbook-style chronological ledgers with debit, credit, and running balance.
- **1-Tap WhatsApp Reminders:** Send polite payment reminders and account statements directly over WhatsApp.

#### 🏦 Bank Accounts & Cash Passbook
- Manage all your accounts in one dashboard: **Cash in Hand**, **SBI**, **HDFC**, **ICICI**, **Axis**, **PNB**, **Bank of Baroda**, **Kotak**, and more.
- **Transfer Money (Cash ↔ Bank):** Shift funds neutrally between accounts without affecting your profit calculations.
- **Passbook Reconciliation:** Compare Ledgerly book balance with your real bank statement balance in one tap.

#### 📦 Inventory & Low Stock Alerts
- Track stock quantities and inventory valuation automatically.
- Sales automatically reduce stock; purchase bills automatically add stock.
- **Amber Warning Strip:** Never run out of your bestselling goods with instant low-stock notifications.

#### 🧾 Professional Invoices & GST Bills
- Create professional Sale Invoices, Purchase Bills, Quotations, and Delivery Challans.
- Customize with your business logo, GSTIN, discounts, item lines, and payment terms.
- Export clean PDF invoices and share directly with customers via WhatsApp or print.

#### 📊 Instant Reports (PDF & Excel)
- **Profit & Loss Statement (P&L):** See your exact shop profits excluding transfers.
- **Day Book:** View all daily transactions grouped chronologically.
- **Aging Analysis:** Track credit duration (0–30 days, 31–60 days, 60+ days) to recover dues faster.
- **Simplified Balance Sheet & Expense Breakdown:** Know your total business net worth.
- Export all reports to **PDF** and **Excel (.xlsx)** with one tap.

#### 🔒 100% Offline-First & Private
- Works without internet. Your data is stored securely on your device.
- Export & restore full JSON backups anytime.
- Secure 4-digit PIN lock for complete privacy.
- Supports **English**, **हिन्दी (Hindi)**, and **ગુજરાતી (Gujarati)**.
